## 一、概览

特劳特的短篇写作与其著作同构，但更像"战地通报"：**先给判断，再给历史，最后给清单**。他极少铺垫气氛，几乎不在开头写"随着……的发展"这类过渡句；开篇第一段往往就是一句判决（"It's futile trying to change minds in the marketplace."），或用一则轶事把读者拽进场景（1969 年那篇以农夫指路开场）。

三个稳定特征：

1. **短句作锤**。样本平均句长 12.6 词，46.2% 的句子不足 12 词；关键论点常压到 4–9 词一句（"They don't need buzz." / "So much for companies being rational." / "Blow the bugles, the positioning era has begun."）。
2. **二元对撞**。句式骨架高度依赖"不是 A，而是 B"：not what you want to do / what your competition will let you do；not the same / one of its kind；strong somewhere / weak everywhere。否定词密度 14.3/千词，约为模糊限制词的 2.8 倍。
3. **口语化的权威感**。第一人称密度 14.3/千词，用"我在这一行 40 年""我曾被请去……"把断言锚在个人经验上；对读者直呼 "you"（21.4/千词），并频繁插入 "make no mistake about it" "How about you?" 这类当面说话的插入语。

一个容易被忽略的演变：**1969–1972 年的特劳特更俏皮、更爱讲段子、更爱用第一人称做事例；2000 年后的短文更像格言、段落更短、转折更硬——但句子本身未必更短**（2016 年样本平均句长反而升到 18.3 词，见 §3 句长漂移表）。幽默则从"讲段子"收缩为"括号里的冷刺"。

## 五、句式与节奏

### 5.1 结论先行，还是铺垫？
**分两种体裁，规律很清楚：**

- **专栏/短文：结论先行。** 第一句就是判决，第二句给理由，第三句给反例。
  - "It's futile trying to change minds in the marketplace. For example: …"（BSI, 2009-07-08）
  - "Suddenly, everyone is running their mouths about word-of-mouth marketing."（Forbes, 2006-03-07）
  - "I've been in the marketing business for over 40 years. I've seen the good old times and the difficult new times. When people ask me what has changed, my response is one word: competition."（BSI, 2007-09-13）
- **长文/演讲式文章：轶事开场，但 3 段内必须落到论点。** 1969 年那篇开头用三段农夫与旅人的对话，紧接着就是一句 "That happens to be the moral of this article."

他**不写**"随着市场竞争的日益激烈"式铺垫；也不写文献综述。历史回顾（50 年代 USP → 60 年代 image → 70 年代 positioning）是他唯一允许的长铺垫，而且一定是**三段式断代**，每段一个时代、一个代表人物、一句判决（1972）。

### 5.2 短句排比
有，但**不是诗性排比，而是"清单式排比"**：同一句法连续砸三到五下，制造压迫感。
- "There are just too many products, too many companies and too much marketing 'noise.'"（1969）
- "There is television… There's radio… There is outdoor… There are newspapers. Direct mail. There are mass magazines. Class magazines. Enthusiast magazines…"（1969，连续 12 个短句/残句）
- "Rather than staying focused on being strong somewhere, they opt for being weak everywhere."（2012，对偶而非排比）

### 5.3 "The problem is…" / "The answer is…"
**这是他的高频思维脚手架，且几乎总是"问题—答案"两拍：**
- "The problem is that most of the buzz was negative."（Forbes, 2006-03-07）
- "All right, here's the answer in three letters: The CEO."（BSI, 2012-12-20）
- "So what to do? The answer is to realize that the CEO is the ultimate CMO."（同上）
- "How do you find the proper direction? … Here is a four-step process to pursue:"（Forbes, 2006-07-03）

样本内 "the problem is" 出现 1 次、"the answer is" 0 次；样本外的 Forbes 2006-07-03 与 BSI 2012-12-20 则直接使用该对句（"The answer is to realize…" / "here's the answer in three letters"）。**他的典型段落是"设问 → 短答 → 编号展开"。**

### 5.4 数字清单
极度偏爱，且清单前必有一句"许可证"式的过渡：
- "…I'd like to offer you four simple rules for playing the game:"（1969）
- "These survival tips will help lead the way:"（2007）
- "Here is a four-step process to pursue:"（2006）
- "The following commandments do not come from a mountaintop."（2012）

清单条目本身也遵守"标题句 + 一句解释 + 一个品牌案例"的三件套（McDonald's/Burger King、Toyota/Detroit、Avis/Hertz、Volvo/safety、A1 酱）。

### 5.5 反问收尾？
**基本不。** 样本中 3 个问句，两个用于段落中段推进，一个用于收尾但立刻自答：
- "How many people really want to chatter about products? Do you really want to talk about your toothpaste or your toilet paper?"（2006，两个连问，段中）
- "Now I ask you, dear reader, does that definition fit what you see on the media you watch? For a few advertisements, maybe, but for the most, not at all."（2016，段中自答）

他真正的收尾方式是**冷句**（cold close）：一句 5–8 词的判词，或一个反讽祝愿。
- "So much for companies being rational."（2007）
- "If you did, all I would say is 'good luck'."（2006）
- "You can't get there from here."（1969，全文出现 5 次，作叠句回环）
- "Should Advertising Age become Storytelling Age? Probably not, but…"（2016，唯一一次用问句收，但立刻自答）

## 六、幽默方式

**结论：冷幽默 + 讽刺 + 括号里的暗刺；几乎不自嘲，绝不搞笑。** 幽默服务于"树敌"，不是服务于亲和。

| 类型 | 原文例证 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|
| 冷面讽刺（deadpan） | "The great copywriters of yesterday, who have gone to that big agency in the sky, would die all over again if they saw some of the campaigns currently running (successful campaigns, we might add)." | Advertising Age, 1972 |
| 括号暗刺 | "(The eighth company in a given field is out of luck.)"；"(Some day someone might even come up with a rating system for politicians.)" | Advertising Age, 1972 |
| 幸灾乐祸（克制的） | "With two major computer manufacturers folding one right after another, the urge to say, 'I told you so,' was irresistible." | Advertising Age, 1972 |
| 夸张比喻 | "What I thought was a competitive marketplace looks like a tea party today."；"the exciting, go-go years of the middle '60s were like a marketing orgy."；"The wreckage is still washing up on the beach." | BSI 2007；Ad Age 1972 |
| 冷面威胁式玩笑 | "You're a lot better off to open up a new front or position -- that is, unless you enjoy being shot-up."；"As a rule, when it comes to building strong programs, trust no one, especially product managers." | Industrial Marketing, 1969 |
| 反讽祝愿 | "If you did, all I would say is 'good luck'." | Forbes, 2006 |
| 装傻式反问 | "Do you really want to talk about your toothpaste or your toilet paper?" | Forbes, 2006 |
| 死亡玩笑（对下属/高管） | "I'm not sure what happened to that young executive but he was never heard from again."；"The Chief Financial Officer's solution is often to kill both halves thus increasing earnings." | BSI, 2012-12-20 |
| 伪经文 | "The following commandments do not come from a mountaintop." | BSI, 2012-09-04 |
| 生造词挖苦 | "perhaps we should drop the word creativity and replace it with 'Dramativity.'" | BSI, 2016-04-22 |

**自嘲**在本批文本中仅见一处、且是策略性的："If you're like me, you're probably a little confused about all this, so let's put some things into perspective."（Forbes, 2006）——先把自己放进"被术语搞晕的人"里，再顺手把整个行业嘲笑一遍。

**他不幽默的时候**：谈战争隐喻、谈"心智"机制、谈 CEO 责任时，一律正色，不给玩笑留缝。

### 7.2 中文互联网流传、但本次未核到英文原句的说法（伪引语/误传警示）

> 判定标准：在本次抓取的 9 篇一手短文（约 9,000 词）与可核书目信息中，**找不到对应英文句子**；或能找到更早/更可靠的另一作者出处。以下均**不建议**在 Skill 中作为"特劳特原话"直接引用。
> 方法限制：受"禁止使用知乎/公众号/百科"约束，**未能逐条追溯这些中文说法的传播链**，因此判定只针对"是否存在可核英文原句"，不等于断言"某中文作者伪造"。凡标"疑伪"者，含义是**无法证实**，引用风险高。

| 中文流传说法 | 最接近的可核英文 | 判定 |
|---|---|---|
| "简单是终极的复杂"（常被安到《简单的力量》头上） | 特劳特侧的可核表述是 "Complexity is the enemy of clear thinking."（官网，B 级）；"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication" 广为归给达·芬奇，非特劳特 | **误传/张冠李戴**，不要引 |
| "定位不是改变产品，而是改变心智" | "Positioning is not what you do to a product. Positioning is what you do to the mind of the prospect."（Positioning, 1981, p.2；见 01-writings.md，两处二手互证） | **意译**，意思对但不是逐字原话；引用应回原句 |
| "如果你不能成为第一，就创造一个品类，让自己成为第一" | 最接近的是《22条商规》品类定律（Ries & Trout, 1993）；该书为**合著**，且中文常省略里斯 | **合著观点被独归特劳特**；引用须署两人 |
| "营销的本质是认知战，不是产品战" | "Marketing is a battle of perceptions, not products."（BSI, 2012-09-04；亦为《22条商规》认知定律） | **可核**（英文原句存在），但中文版常丢掉"perceptions"的心理学含义，译成"认知战" |
| "品牌是品类的代表，品类是品牌的根基" | 本次一手短篇中**无此句**；"品类"战略的系统表述主要来自里斯系（Ries & Ries）与张云 | **疑伪/归属错误**，高概率不是特劳特原话 |
| "定位一旦确立，就不要改变" | 特劳特恰恰强调 "repositioning"（2009 书名即 *Repositioning*）；原句未见 | **与本人观点相反**，属误传 |
| "做品牌就是做第一" | 本次一手短文中**无此句**；《22条商规》有"领先定律"章节名，但"better to be first"的英文原句本次未核到 | **压缩式改写 + 出处未核**；不要当引语 |

另需注意：中文版《定位》的副标题"争夺用户心智的战争"是**出版社文案**，不是特劳特写的句子；不要当作引语。

## 八、禁忌表达（他不用的词与腔调）

**有直接证据的：**

1. **造词与术语堆叠**。他对营销新词的态度是公开嘲讽，而非中立：
   > "Now we have a new dictionary to learn. Word-of-mouth is now buzz marketing, viral marketing, community marketing, grassroots marketing, evangelist marketing, product seeding, influencer marketing, cause marketing, conversation creation, brand blogging and referral programs. That's the good stuff."（Forbes, 2006-03-07）
   紧接着用 "What isn't so good is stealth marketing, shilling, infiltration, comment spam…" 把新词表变成讽刺清单。**他不接受"术语更新=进步"。**

2. **"creativity" 作为行业黑话**。他多次把 creativity 放进引号或加 "so-called"：
   > "Quite often that strategy can disappear in a cloud of so-called creativity."（BSI, 2012-12-20）
   > "The fun and games of the '60s have given way to the harsh realities of the '70s."（Ad Age, 1972）
   > "perhaps we should drop the word creativity and replace it with 'Dramativity.'"（BSI, 2016，反讽）

3. **研究腔与"事实崇拜"**。
   > "Marketing people are preoccupied with research and 'getting the facts.' … This is an illusion. There is no objective reality."（BSI, 2012-09-04）
   他把 "getting the facts" 打上引号，把 research 描述为" preoccupation"（癖好），而非方法。

4. **管理畅销书话语**。他点名挖苦 "In Search of Excellence" 与 "Built to Last"：
   > "In retrospect, a better title for the book might have been *In Search of Strategy*."（Forbes, 2006-07-03）
   > "they write glowingly about 'Big Hairy Audacious Goals'"（同上，"glowingly" 是贬义用法）

**可验证的"用词缺席"（负面证据）：**
在 982 词的 20 段样本中，以下词出现次数均为 **0**：synergy、synergies、holistic、paradigm、leverage、ecosystem、disrupt/disruption、engagement、stakeholder、best practice、actionable。本次读过的 9 篇短文中亦未见到它们（未做全量词频核验，仅人工扫读）。它们不是"他声明禁用"，而是**其常用语域里基本不出现**。写作时应视为风格红线：一旦出现，语气立刻不像特劳特。

**他会用的替代词（均在本批文本中出现）**：nonsense（书名副题）、so-called、me-too、illusion、reality（"There is no objective reality."）；书名层面则用 the obvious、simplicity。

**信息不足**：未找到特劳特本人**明确列出一份"禁用词清单"**的文字（如"我从不用 synergy 这个词"）。以上均为**文本行为证据**，不是他的自我声明，引用时不要写成"特劳特禁止使用 synergy"。

## 十、给后续 Skill 写作的三条可执行结论

1. **写"特劳特腔"时，先把结论压到 12 词以内，再解释。** 段落开头用判决句，不用背景句；结论后立刻给一个具体品牌案例（McDonald's、Avis、Volvo、Xerox、Segway 这类"众所周知的名字"）。
2. **节奏靠"but"和句号，不靠形容词。** 关键段落里放一次硬转折（but，不用 however）；长句之后跟一个 ≤8 词的短句收束；不用破折号，少用分号。
3. **幽默只放在括号、脚注和收尾冷句里。** 正文论证保持严肃；讽刺对象是"行业共识、咨询黑话、CEO 的自我欺骗"，不是读者。
